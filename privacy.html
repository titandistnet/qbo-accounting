Privacy Policy

Titan Distribution Network

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Purpose

Titan Distribution Network is developing an internal accounting integration that connects authorized QuickBooks Online company accounts to Microsoft Excel for bookkeeping, financial reporting, account reconciliation support, and intercompany transaction analysis. The application is intended for authorized internal business use.

2. Information Accessed

Depending on the functions enabled, the application may access company information, chart of accounts, vendor records, bills, bill payments, bank-account ledger transactions, journal entries, and other accounting records through the Intuit QuickBooks Online API. These records may contain business contact information and financial information.

3. Use of Information

Information is used to prepare internal accounting reports, monitor outstanding payables, analyze intercompany balances, and support bookkeeping and reconciliation activities. The application is not intended to sell QuickBooks data or use it for advertising.

4. Data Storage and Security

The planned integration uses a locally operated data-processing program and Microsoft Excel workbooks. Authorized personnel are responsible for protecting the computer, workbook files, and API credentials. OAuth access and refresh tokens must be stored securely and must not be published in public repositories. Additional security controls will be documented and implemented before live production data is connected.

5. Information Sharing

Accounting data is intended for authorized internal business purposes. Information may be disclosed when required by law or to service providers necessary for authorized operations, subject to applicable agreements and safeguards.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Accounting records and exported reports are retained according to applicable business, tax, legal, and recordkeeping requirements. Authorized personnel may remove locally stored integration data when no longer needed, subject to those obligations. Disconnecting the application does not automatically delete existing QuickBooks records or previously generated Excel files.

7. Access and Changes

Access to the integration is intended to be restricted to authorized personnel. Titan Distribution Network may revise this policy as the integration is implemented or its data practices change.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact Titan Distribution Network through its authorized business administration channels.
